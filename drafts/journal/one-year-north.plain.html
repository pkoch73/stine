Oslo

One year north

Jan 2026 · 8 min

A year ago I landed in Oslo with two suitcases and a very rough plan. The Norwegian is coming slower than I'd like — I've restarted the same app three times — but the trails, the light, and the particular quiet of a Nordic winter have made up for it.

The thing nobody told me about moving somewhere for graduate school is how much of it is just learning to be a person in a new place, one grocery store and one bus route at a time.
