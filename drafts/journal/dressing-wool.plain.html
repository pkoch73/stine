Dressing wool: a season at Dale

Mar 2026 · 7 min

Working the floor at the Dale of Norway flagship has taught me more about how people actually wear wool than any pattern book. Tourists want the lusekofte for the photo. Norwegians want it for the bus stop in February.

The best styling advice I give is the most boring: buy one size up, layer it over something thin, and stop treating wool like it's precious. It's meant to be worn hard and it gets better with age.
