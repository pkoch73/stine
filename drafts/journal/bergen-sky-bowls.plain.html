Bowls the colour of a Bergen sky

Feb 2026 · 5 min

I threw eleven bowls this month and kept four. The glaze I was chasing — a flat, cool grey with a little iron speckle — took six tests to get right, and even then only one kiln load came out the colour I wanted.

It's the same grey as the sky over Bergen the one weekend I visited: not dramatic, just quietly complete.
