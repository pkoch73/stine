Trails & Snow

First snow at Sognsvann

Nov 2025 · 4 min

The lake froze before I was ready for it. One week I was running the loop in shoes, the next the trail was white and everyone else already had skis on.

I raced cross-country through most of high school and college, and I thought I'd left the sport behind when I hung up my racing bib. But the first real snow at Sognsvann made it clear I hadn't — I was back on classic skis within the hour, badly out of practice and thrilled about it.
