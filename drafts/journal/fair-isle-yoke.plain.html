A Fair Isle yoke, decoded

Apr 2026 · 9 min

I spent a semester staring at marginalia in illuminated manuscripts before I ever picked up double-pointed needles, and I didn't expect the two to talk to each other. But a Fair Isle yoke is built the same way a manuscript border is: a repeating unit, a strict grid, and just enough variation to prove a human hand made it.

This sweater took me four attempts to get the colour dominance right. The yarn is Norwegian wool, undyed where I could find it, and every round reminded me of counting syllables in Old Norse verse — same discipline, different needles.
