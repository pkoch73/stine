About

Hei, I'm Stine.

I grew up racing on cross-country skis before trading my poles for an English degree at Bates College in Maine, where I found my way into medieval literature almost by accident and never left. Last year I moved to Oslo to begin a master's in Nordic Studies, with an eye toward a PhD — chasing sagas and manuscripts in the language they were written in.

Outside of school, I work the floor at the Dale of Norway flagship store, where I get to talk wool and colourwork with strangers all day — a job that feeds the same part of me that knits, throws pots, and paints on the weekends. I still run and ski whenever Oslo's trails let me, and I'm slowly getting better at cycling in the rain.

This site is where all of it lives together: the reading, the making, the miles, and the wardrobe advice I can't help but give.
